# Information architecture

| Experience | Primary question | Hierarchy |
|---|---|---|
| Team | How are we performing? | Current context, offense, defense, upcoming opponents |
| Player | Who contributes and how? | Roster picker, cinematic hero, position-specific and situational analytics |
| Game | What happened and why? | Game selector, score, possession timeline, drive detail, schedule |
| Matchup | What should we expect? | Next opponent or historical fallback, comparison, tendencies |

Team is the default. Shared season context and sticky four-tab navigation sit above content. Classic and lab remain distinct routes. Historical data availability must constrain the experience.

Planned: identity metadata within hero negative space; Beyond the Numbers after core analytics; Building the Bears in Team; Football IQ contextual disclosures. These are approved concepts, not verified production features. Preserve analytical hierarchy and avoid adding navigation solely for editorial content.
