# Art direction

Cinematic, unofficial editorial imagery: navy/orange, smoke, tactical playbook marks and recognizable football gestures. Avoid duplicated names or taglines already rendered in UI. Prefer helmeted action poses; portraits only when supported by reliable references. Do not imply photographic authenticity for generated artwork.

Use WebP, target 1200×1080 and approximately 350 KB where feasible (targets, not enforced limits). File names normalized lowercase first-last-number.webp, including zero-padded single digits, then name-only. Existing Caleb legacy art remains a fallback; ESPN headshot and neutral placeholder follow. Player IDs, not filenames, identify analytics. Verify each image, jersey and pose with roster context; no unverified nickname or personal story. Rights and attribution review before wider distribution.

Evidence: PR #37, retrospective #63, artwork backlog #44.
