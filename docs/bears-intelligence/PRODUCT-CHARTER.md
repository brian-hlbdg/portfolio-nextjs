# Product charter

## Vision
Make football performance, people, history and strategy understandable through interactive intelligence and editorial storytelling. Give casual fans an accessible entry point and analytical fans defensible detail.

## Positioning
Bears Intelligence is a product in its own right, not merely a portfolio page. Its four pillars are performance, identity, history and strategy. It is an independent unofficial, currently noncommercial experience; do not imply NFL or team endorsement.

## Principles
- Explain the game, not just display numbers.
- Preserve fan identity through cinematic/editorial design without sacrificing readability.
- Prefer sourced, verifiable facts; no invented player biographies, tracking coordinates or rankings.
- Preserve season context, missing values and source limitations.
- Design mobile-first interactions and progressively enhance complex analytics.
- Prefer modular static architecture until requirements justify added infrastructure.

## Scope and boundaries
Team, Player, Game and Matchup are the core experiences. Football IQ, Beyond the Numbers, management, historical exploration and forecasts are staged enhancements. No accounts, paid data, commercial launch or separate repository are assumed. Review source rights, image rights and branding before distribution or monetization.

## Success measures
Reliability: successful refreshes and recoverable failures. Integrity: reconciled sample metrics and explicit coverage. Usability: ability to find a player/game and explain a metric without repeated scrolling. Accessibility: keyboard/touch and screen-reader checks. Performance: measured bundle/LCP and data-transfer budgets. Engagement: optional future measurement with privacy review. These are goals, not measured achievements.

Product direction and prioritization: repository owner. AI-assisted implementation must be reviewed and verified. See epics #81–88.
